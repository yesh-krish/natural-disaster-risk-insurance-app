      Heat Index is a screening estimate, not WBGT. For outdoor construction, use on-site WBGT with workload, clothing/PPE and acclimatization guidance. No construction shutdown threshold is supplied. Heat illness can occur below the listed bands. Sun and local heat sources can increase exposure.
