      Satellite fire observations — NASA FIRMS (VIIRS)

      Hot spots detected by the NOAA-20 and NOAA-21 satellites in about the last 24 hours, in ~375 m pixels. Marker colour shows age: red under 6 hours, orange under 12 hours, amber older.

      Select a location to check recent satellite fire detections.

        A detection means something hot was seen from space, not a confirmed wildfire: prescribed or farm burns, gas flares and industry can appear too. No detection does not mean no fire, because clouds, smoke and small or short fires can be missed. Fire radiative power (MW) is not fireline intensity (kW/m). Confirm with agency incident reports.
