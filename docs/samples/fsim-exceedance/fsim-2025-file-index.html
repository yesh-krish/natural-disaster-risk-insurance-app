Spatial datasets of probabilistic wildfire risk components for the conterminous United States (270m) for circa 2011 climate and projected future climate circa 2047

Research Data Publication File Index

File Folder Description
_metadata_RDS-2025-0006.html   Metadata file in HTML format containing a description of the content, quality, and other characteristics of the data.
_metadata_RDS-2025-0006.xml   Metadata file in Extensible Markup Language (XML) format containing a description of the content, quality, and other characteristics of the data.
_variable_descriptions.csv \Data Comma-separated values (CSV) file containing a list and description of variables found in all data files.

FLP3.tif \Data\2011ClimateRun GeoTIFF containing 2011 climate conditional flame length probability for the conterminous United States in FIL 3 (4 < 6 feet).
FLP4.tif \Data\2011ClimateRun GeoTIFF containing 2011 climate conditional flame length probability for the conterminous United States in FIL 4 (6 < 8 feet).
FLP5.tif \Data\2011ClimateRun GeoTIFF containing 2011 climate conditional flame length probability for the conterminous United States in FIL 5 (8 < 12 feet).
FLP6.tif \Data\2011ClimateRun GeoTIFF containing 2011 climate conditional flame length probability for the conterminous United States in FIL 6 (12+ feet).
BP.tif \Data\2047ClimateRun GeoTIFF containing 2047 climate annual burn probability for the conterminous United States.
FLP1.tif \Data\2047ClimateRun GeoTIFF containing 2047 climate conditional flame length probability for the conterminous United States in FIL 1 (< 2 feet).
FLP2.tif \Data\2047ClimateRun GeoTIFF containing 2047 climate conditional flame length probability for the conterminous United States in FIL 2 (2 < 4 feet).
FLP3.tif \Data\2047ClimateRun GeoTIFF containing 2047 climate conditional flame length probability for the conterminous United States in FIL 3 (4 < 6 feet).
FLP4.tif \Data\2047ClimateRun GeoTIFF containing 2047 climate conditional flame length probability for the conterminous United States in FIL 4 (6 < 8 feet).
FLP5.tif \Data\2047ClimateRun GeoTIFF containing 2047 climate conditional flame length probability for the conterminous United States in FIL 5 (8 < 12 feet).
FLP6.tif \Data\2047ClimateRun GeoTIFF containing 2011 climate conditional flame length probability for the conterminous United States in FIL 6 (12+ feet).
county_change.csv \Data\CountyChange CSV file containing a table of U.S. counties with mean and median BP values of the 2011 climate and 2047 climate-conditioned run, mean values of low-intensity and high-intensity conditional FLP, and percent change between these runs. (This same content is available in counties.gdb.)
counties.gdb \Data\CountyChange Esri file geodatabase (GDB) containing a feature class (counties_NatFSim2020_CC) of U.S. counties including mean BP and intensity of the 2011 climate (2020-landscape) and projected 2047 climate run. (This same content is available in counties_change.csv.)
pyrome_change.csv \Data\PyromeChange CSV file containing a table of pyromes with mean and median BP values of the 2011 climate and 2047 climate-conditioned run, mean values of low-intensity and high-intensity conditional FLP, and percent change between these runs. (This same content is available in pyromes.gdb.)
pyromes.gdb \Data\PyromeChange File geodatabase containing a feature class (pyromes_NatFSim2020_CC) of pyromes including mean BP and intensity of the 2011 climate (2020-landscape) and projected 2047 climate run. (This same content is available in pyrome_change.csv.)
